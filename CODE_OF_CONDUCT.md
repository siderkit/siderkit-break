# Code of conduct

## Our commitment

Siderkit Break is a small project. We want it to be a place where people can report bugs, suggest improvements, translate the interface, and ask questions without being harassed or dismissed.

## Expected behavior

- Be direct and kind. Critique the work, not the person.
- Assume good intent, but take responsibility for the effect of your words.
- Keep discussions focused on the problem and the smallest useful change.
- Respect different platforms, languages, time zones, and experience levels.
- Ask before sharing someone else's private information, logs, or screenshots.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, insults, or sustained disruption.
- Discriminatory comments about race, ethnicity, nationality, gender, gender identity, sexual orientation, disability, age, or religion.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Spam, unwanted promotion, or repeated off-topic comments.
- Retaliation against someone who reports a problem.

## Scope

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and any other project space. It also applies when someone represents the project in public.

## Reporting

If you experience or witness unacceptable behavior, contact the repository maintainers privately through GitHub. Do not open a public issue with personal details. Reports will be reviewed as confidentially as the platform allows.

Maintainers may edit or remove comments, close threads, or block accounts when behavior crosses these lines. Enforcement decisions should be proportionate and explained when possible.

## Attribution

This is a short project-specific code of conduct, not a license to copy another community's policy. It may be updated as the project grows.
